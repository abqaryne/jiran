# Jiran: Survey Questions (Google Form draft)

Target: **100+ UM students** who live off campus or are looking for housing. Time to complete: about 6–8 minutes.
Format: anonymous. Put an English and a Malay version in the same form (language switch at the start).
Do a 5-person pilot first and fix confusing questions before sending it out.

Question types: **SC** = single choice, **MC** = multiple choice, **N** = number, **LS** = 1–5 scale, **R** = ranking, **T** = short text.

---

## Section A. Consent and screening (everyone)

**A1. Consent (SC, required).** This survey is for the WIA2007 group project at Universiti Malaya. It is anonymous and voluntary, takes about 7 minutes, and answers are used only for the project report and app design (PDPA 2010). You can stop at any time. *Do you agree to take part?* Yes / No → "No" ends the form.

**A2. Are you currently a UM student? (SC)** Yes / No → "No" ends the form.

**A3. Where do you live now? (SC)**
- Off campus (rented room or unit)
- Residential college on campus
- With family or at home (commuting)
- Looking for a place, not settled yet

*Skip logic:* "Off campus" → sections B–G in full. "Looking for a place" → B, D, E, F, G, H (skip C). "Residential college" or "with family" → B, E, F, H, I (skip C, D, G).

## Section H. Features, trust and AI (everyone)

**H1. How important is each feature to you? (LS 1 = not important, 5 = very important, for each)**
- Listings only from verified UM students
- Search filter by maximum travel time to my faculty
- Seeing the true monthly cost (rent + utilities + commute) before choosing
- Comparing ways to commute (bus/LRT + shuttle, e-hailing, driving) with time and cost
- Seeing peak vs off-peak time and cost
- Step-by-step public transport route from the room to my faculty
- Map view of available rooms
- Saving favourite listings
- Reviews of the building (water, internet, security, noise)
- Roommate matching with reasons
- Checking a tenancy agreement for risky clauses
- Splitting rent and bills with housemates
- Searching in plain English or Malay (e.g. "room under RM550, near LRT")

## Practical notes

- **Sampling:** aim for spread across faculties and areas, not only FSKTM. Send through UMSU, PEKOM, faculty groups and Telegram rental channels (ask the admins for permission first).
- **Pilot first:** have 5 students complete it, time them, and cut questions if it takes more than 8 minutes.
- **Keep the evidence:** export the anonymised results and charts for the report, and record which answer changed which requirement (the report needs a "finding to FR" table).
- **Never ask** for name, student ID, phone number or exact address in the main form. Interview contact details in I3 go in a separate sheet.
