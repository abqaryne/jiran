## Description

People leave. The room must become vacant again and what is owed must not disappear. If the Host leaves they hand the Host role to another member; if nobody accepts, the listing closes and the household stays.

## Work needed

- Move-out screen for a member.
- The room becomes vacant at its previous price; the Host may change that price as long as all prices do not exceed the Unit rent; other members' prices change only if each accepts.
- The leaver's unsettled balance stays recorded as owed until settled; they keep read-only access to their own history.
- Host hand-over flow.
- When the last member leaves or the tenancy ends, close the household (E7.2).
