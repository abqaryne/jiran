## Description

House rules (quiet hours, guests, cleaning) only work if everyone accepted them. P1: the household keeps a list of rules that each member must accept and sees who has. Changing a rule resets every acceptance. Stored in `households.rules` as `{text, acceptedBy}`.

The Household keeps a short list of rules (quiet hours, guests, cleaning). Each member accepts every rule; the screen shows who has accepted. Editing a rule's text clears all acceptances of that rule and notifies the members.
