## Description

Fair chore rotation avoids arguments. P1: a household defines recurring chores and the system rotates the assignment, reminds the person whose turn it is by push notification, and lets them mark it done. Data: `chores` (task, frequency daily/weekly/biweekly, rotation, currentUid, dueDate, done).

The household sets a chore (for example Kitchen cleaning), how often it repeats (daily, weekly or biweekly) and the order of members. When the due date passes or the chore is done, the next member in the rotation becomes `currentUid` and `dueDate` moves forward.

## Work needed

- Rotation logic in plain Java that skips no one and repeats fairly, with JUnit.
- Chores screen: list, add, mark done.
- Reminders through FCM (E5.4).
- Rules: only members read or write chores.
- Rotation function: given `rotation`, `currentUid`, `frequency` and the completion date, return the next `currentUid` and `dueDate`.
- Handle a member leaving the household (remove from `rotation`, keep the fairness order) and a member joining.
- Mark done sets `done: true` until the next cycle.
