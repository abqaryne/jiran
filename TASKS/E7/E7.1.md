## Description

Shared expenses are only trusted if the balances are exact. The logic is plain Java and unit-tested. Rent is split by each room's price from the Unit; utilities and other shared expenses are split equally unless the user picks a custom split. The app only calculates and tracks who owes whom; it does not process payments.

## Work needed

- Models: `Expense` (amountSen, paidByUid, splitAmong with shareSen, date, settled), `Household`.
- `computeBalances()`: net balance per member; balances over all members sum to zero.
- Settle-up that minimises the number of transfers.
- Split modes: equal, by amount, by percent; rounding remainder policy in sen.
- JUnit: balances sum to zero, rounding, 2 and 4 members, a member who paid nothing.
