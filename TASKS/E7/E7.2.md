## Description

Household data (expenses, chores, rules, members' timetables) is readable and writable only by that household's members. A Household is created when a Group's tenancy starts, or joined by invite code. A closed household is kept read-only for 6 months so balances can be settled, then deleted.

## Work needed

- `households` (memberUids, hostUid, listingId, inviteCode, rules, status, closedAt) and `expenses`, `chores` documents with rules for members only.
- Create a Household from a Group (same members and room prices) or join with an invite code.
- Closing sets `status: 'closed'` and `closedAt`; the 6-month deletion job.
- Rules: members only; a leaver keeps read-only access to their own balance history.
- Emulator tests for each denied case.
