## Description

The match score needs the student's answers to seven questions. The shapes are fixed by Aaron's `match.js` and the schema (`matchProfiles/{uid}.answers`): sleep as minutes after midnight (0 to 1439), cleanliness, noise and cooking as 0 to 4, smoking and pets as yes/no, budget as `{minSen, maxSen}`.

The questions should match the survey wording (`docs/product/SURVEY.md`) so students answer what they know.

- Save to `matchProfiles/{uid}` (owner only); retaking replaces the old answers and the score.
- Reuse the budget already given in the profile (E1.3) as the default.
- Tests for saving answers and for out-of-range values.

## Constraints

- Similarity is not a hard filter for smoking or pets.
- Gender, if added, is a hard filter and not a score factor.
- The weights are placeholders until the survey (assumption #9).
- Seven factors, fixed by Aaron's `match.js`.
- Gender is not part of the match score. The housemate page has the same filter chip as Rooms (Male / Female / Any, default Any).
- The questions follow the survey as it was sent (T1.1); the survey does not change.
