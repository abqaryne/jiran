## Description

After a Connection two students arrange a viewing and the Host may share the tenancy agreement. The chat is `chats/{chatId}` with `participantUids` and a `messages` subcollection (senderUid, text, attachmentType, attachmentUrl, sentAt).

The tenancy agreement is confidential: it reaches the student only inside the chat, and the contract check on it is private to that student.

## Work needed

- Chat screen: message list in order, text input, photo attachment (a free-host URL).
- A **+** button in the composer opens the drawer "Add to this chat" with three actions: Arrange a viewing, Upload a contract (opens the private Contract check upload, E8.3) and Form a group (opens Create a group with this Connection already invited, or Open group when the student has one, E9.2). Nothing is pushed into the thread as a prompt card (Ary, 11 Nov 2026).
- Viewing proposal card: Arrange a viewing opens a sheet with a Date field and a Time field (a date picker and a time picker, 24-hour, no fixed time slots, dates in the past disabled), and sending it posts the card; the other person accepts or changes it.
- Private agreement card: the Host shares the agreement in the chat; only the two people can open it; 'Run contract check' opens E8.3.
- Rules (Gathan): only participants read or write messages; a contract file shared in a chat is private to the two people.
- Chat retention follows the schema (counted from the Connection ending).

## Constraints

- The schema says contract files are processed ephemerally and not stored, while the PRD says uploads auto-delete after 30 days; reconcile before E8.3.
- `chats` is `[PROPOSED]` in the schema.
- Chat attachments are photos only, resized on the phone to at most 1600 px and 2 MB, uploaded through the same Cloudinary flow as listings (E2.1). Contract files never go into a chat.

## Acceptance (how QA checks it)

- [ ] A viewing proposal card can be accepted or changed.

- [ ] A contract file shared in the chat is readable only by the two people.
