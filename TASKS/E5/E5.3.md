## Description

A person becomes a Household member only after the Host marks their room occupied and they confirm their move-in. Before that they only have a Connection with the Host, so shared expenses never include someone who has not moved in. The tenancy itself is signed outside the app.

## Work needed

- Host marks a room occupied: sets `rooms.status = 'occupied'` and `occupiedByUid`, adds the tenant to `residentUids`.
- Tenant sees a confirm-move-in card and confirms.
- Only after both steps is the Household created with the same members (E7.2) and the Unit tab unlocks.
- If the last vacant room becomes occupied, the listing closes (E2.3).
- Rules: a tenant who has not confirmed has no access to household data.

## Constraints

- A room may be occupied by someone with no Jiran account; they appear only as 'Occupant of the Master room' with no personal data.
- A Group that agreed a split becomes the Household with the same members and room prices; nothing is re-entered.
