## Description

The demo and the tests need listings, but Jiran never copies other sites' listings or photos and invents no prices (`docs/product/ASSUMPTIONS.md`). Seed listings are fictional but realistic: invented units in real areas (for example Pantai Dalam, Kerinchi) with prices modelled on reference ranges noted by hand from public advertisements, each range with its source and date.

## Work needed

- Note reference prices by hand in a sheet: area, room type, price range, source (site or channel) and date; cite it in the report as reference prices from public advertisements, October 2026.
- Write 10 to 15 fictional listings (rooms of each type, different areas and gender preferences, amenities) and import them with `isSample: true`; the app labels them as sample data.
- Seed `areas` (name, lat, lng, rentLowSen, rentHighSen, nearestStations) and `config/app` (room price weights, utilities estimate).
- A seed script that works against the Firebase emulator (`demo-jiran`) and can be re-run.

## Constraints

- No real names, phones, addresses or photos (PDPA 2010).
- Room price weights come from the team's survey, not from advertisements (assumption #1).
- The utilities estimate in `config/app` is RM62 sample until assumption #7 is verified.

## Acceptance (how QA checks it)

Aly runs these checks and records the result in ClickUp.

- [ ] 10 to 15 listings, each labelled sample data, with fictional hosts and no real addresses or phone numbers.
- [ ] Prices stay inside the reference ranges the team noted, each range with area, room type, source and date.
- [ ] Listings cover different areas and gender preferences, so filters can be tested.
- [ ] No listing text is copied from another site.
