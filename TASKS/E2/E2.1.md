## Description

This is the first half of Flow A (a Host who already rents a Unit opens a vacant Room). A Host is a verified UM student who holds or is about to hold the tenancy; landlords have no account. The Host chooses the Area from the seeded list and types the building name (no Google Places, decided 31 Oct 2026). The Area centre is stored as an estimated location (`coordinateSource: area_center_estimate`) so the commute calculation has a destination; the Building street address is public (`buildingAddress`) and only the exact Unit goes into the private location.

Data: the public `listings/{listingId}` (areaId, buildingName, buildingAddress, placeId, lat, lng, coordinateSource, totalRentSen, depositSen, genderPref, amenities, photoUrls, isSample, status, createdAt, updatedAt, and expiresAt once published) and the private `listings/{id}/private/location` (exactAddress = the Unit, residentUids).

## Constraints

- A listing cannot be published without building name, total rent, deposit, area, one photo and one priced vacant room (the last two are finished in E2.2).
- Money in integer sen: `totalRentSen`, `depositSen`.
- Listings in the same building share the building name, which groups them in the same Building.
- The Host is a UM student: sign-up already guarantees this (E1.1).
- Step 1 saves a Host-only draft (ADR-0009, accepted by Ary 10 Nov 2026); E2.2 publishes it once a priced vacant Room exists.
- No Google Places (it needs billing): the Host chooses the Area from the seeded `areas` list and types the building name. The Area centre is stored with `coordinateSource: area_center_estimate` and labelled as an estimate; the exact Unit address goes only into `private/location`. openrouteservice geocoding only if its free quota fits (assumption #11).
- Photos: no Firebase Storage (it needs the Blaze plan). Photos go to Cloudinary Free through an upload signed by the Worker (the API secret stays in the Worker); Firestore stores the URLs.
