## Description

A Host needs one place to see their listing and its requests, and a listing that nobody maintains must disappear. A listing closes by itself 30 days after posting or last renewal, and when its last vacant room is marked occupied.

Requests are `listingRequests/{listingId}_{requesterUid}` documents (message, status `pending`/`accepted`/`rejected`/`cancelled`); only the Host sets accepted or rejected.

## Work needed

- My listing screen: status, days left, the rooms and their prices, and the incoming requests.
- Renew: sets `expiresAt` = now + 30 days and keeps `status` `active`.
- Closing by itself: either a scheduled job on the Worker or a check when the listing is read (decide with Aaron; Firestore triggers are not on the free plan).
- Mark a room occupied (completed together with the move-in confirmation in E5.3).
- Rules tests: only the owner can write the listing; a student cannot change another's rooms.
