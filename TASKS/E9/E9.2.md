## Description

A group of two to four rents a whole unit together. The app puts the same facts in front of everyone and never decides for the group. Data: `groups` (memberUids, targetListingId/targetEvalId, spots, roomSplit, splitOption, memberStatus, checklist, acceptedBy, status forming/confirmed/dissolved).

## Work needed

- `groups` data and rules.
- Split options in one tap: equal, by room type (default weights), custom; every option adds up exactly to the Unit rent.
- Member status: In, Not sure or Needs changes with a short note; the group is confirmed only when everyone is In.
- Pre-signing checklist (contract checked, deposit discussed, signer chosen): a reminder, not a gate.
- Changing any price resets every acceptance; a member leaving resets them and a group of one dissolves.
- Choose the Host when several sign together.
