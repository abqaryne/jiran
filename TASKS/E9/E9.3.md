## Description

After evaluating several units the group compares them side by side. This is built last of the four decision aids and shows facts, not a single best-unit score.

The compare screen shows the same four facts for every Evaluation: total rent, deposit, each member's Room price and separate one-way commute fare, and each member's travel time to their own faculty. A group sees where the decision is hard, for example 'Unit B costs Rina RM90 less but is 20 minutes farther for Aisyah', but that sentence is a P2 AI summary, not part of this story.

## Work needed

- Compare screen for two or three Evaluations: rent, deposit, every member's Room price and separate one-way commute fare and every member's travel time to their faculty.
- Entries (Figma, 12 Nov 2026): the "Compare units" button on the Group screen, and a "Compare Units" row at the bottom of an Evaluation result ("Room prices"). Both open the compare screen.
- **Add another Unit** page: lists the Units in the comparison (a Listing or an Evaluation, at most 3) with Remove on each, and below it "Evaluate a Unit" (opens the Evaluate form) and "Pick a Listing" (opens Pick a Unit); the primary button goes back to the compare screen. With 3 Units it says to remove one before adding another. Removing only takes a Unit out of the comparison, it never deletes the Evaluation.
- Horizontal comparison of two or three Evaluations with the same row order.
- Highlight, per row, the lowest rent and the shortest commute without crowning a winner.
- Open an Evaluation from the compare screen.
