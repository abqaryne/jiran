## Description

Students want to know what a building is like. The area page shows student-reported rent ranges (`areas.rentLowSen`/`rentHighSen`) and moderated building reviews (water, internet, security, noise). A building can be reviewed only by people who are or were members of a Household in a Unit of that building.

## Work needed

- Area page: rent range, nearest stations, listings and reviews for the area.
- Review form: ratings 1 to 5 for water, internet, security and noise plus text; `reviews` with `moderated: false` by default.
- Only `moderated == true` reviews are public; users can report a review.
- Rules: only past or current residents of that building can write; authors write only their own.
- Moderation: a `moderated` flag set by an admin or the Worker; decide who moderates before building the review list.
- Average rating per criterion (water, internet, security, noise) shown only with at least one moderated review.
