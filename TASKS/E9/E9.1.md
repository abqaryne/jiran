## Description

Friends who already live together, or plan to, can evaluate any unit, including one found on another site, with a short form that works without AI: building name (place search), total rent, number of rooms by type, and optionally a deposit. The system sets a price per room type, shows each member's true monthly cost and each member's commute card to their own faculty. Jiran never fetches or scrapes another site.

## Work needed

- Default split (E2.2 logic) that members can edit; for evaluations the total must equal the rent exactly.
- Each member picks a room; the form blocks two members on one room or prices that do not add up.
- Result screen: true monthly cost and commute card per member.
- Delete after 90 days unless attached to a Group as its Target Unit.
