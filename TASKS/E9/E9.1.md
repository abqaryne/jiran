## Description

Friends who already live together, or plan to, can evaluate any unit, including one found on another site, with a short form that works without AI: building name (place search), total rent, number of rooms by type, and optionally a deposit. The system sets a price per room type, shows each member's Room price and separate one-way commute fare and each member's commute card to their own faculty. Jiran never fetches or scrapes another site.
