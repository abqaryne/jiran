## Description

This is the core of Jiran: for a student and a Listing, how long and how much does it cost to reach class by walking, bus or LRT plus the UM shuttle, e-hailing, and driving alone? Aaron has merged the engine and tests: a Routes adapter, GTFS processing, a configurable pricing layer (`priceRoutes`), the per-trip calculator (`calculatePerTripCommute`), and published-factor CO₂ calculation (`calculateTripEmissions`).

Target rules (Ary, 11 Nov 2026): estimate a one-way trip from the candidate Listing to the faculty without class times, attendance counts or monthly totals. Use integer-sen fares, explain supported transit legs and show missing data explicitly. The old monthly/before-class engine was replaced on 11 Nov 2026 by the per-trip one; see `docs/engineering/api-contracts.md`.

## Constraints

- No timetable, campus-day count, room rent, utilities or monthly-trip input is needed for a commute estimate. No live traffic data on the free plan; there is no peak scenario (removed 11 Nov 2026).
- Assumptions #3 (fares), #4 (car cost), #5 (transit coverage) and #6 (CO₂) must be verified before any number is called more than an estimate.
- The production listing/unit request shape is proposed, not implemented; the local callable only accepts fixture scenarios (`per-trip`, `no-known-fare`, `missing-data`); the internal service takes `{listingId, roomId}` and an optional reference departure, default now.
- Free-only stack: openrouteservice and GTFS.
- The shuttle leg uses the timetable from T1.4 (Dafi); until it exists the shuttle leg is not shown.

## Resolution (30 Oct 2026)

In progress. Aaron merged (commits `[E3.3]`, 27 Oct 2026):

- `commute.js`: monthly calculation from per-trip sen and explicit monthly trips, arrival strictly before class, cheapest on-time option, unavailable reasons.
- `commute-pricing.js`: `priceRoutes` with sourced e-hailing and driving assumptions and BigInt half-up rounding.
