## Description

This is the core of Jiran: for a student and a Listing, how long and how much does it cost to reach class by walking, bus or LRT plus the UM shuttle, e-hailing, and driving alone? Aaron has merged the engine and tests: a Routes adapter, GTFS processing, a configurable pricing layer (`priceRoutes`), the monthly calculator (`calculateCommute`), and published-factor CO₂ calculation (`calculateTripEmissions`).

The rules are in `docs/api-contracts.md`: arrival must be strictly before class; monthly cost = integer per-trip sen × explicit monthly trips; the cheapest on-time available option wins, ties by shorter duration; unknown fares stay unavailable, never zero.

## Work needed

- Routes adapter and GTFS transit data (free: openrouteservice for walking and road, Prasarana GTFS for bus and rail).
- Pricing config: e-hailing formula `(baseSen + metres × rateSenPerKm / 1000) × multiplierBasisPoints / 10000`; driving cost from fuel use, fuel price, parking and tolls.
- UM shuttle timetable as scheduled data (from T1.4); labelled scheduled, not live.
- Label every figure as an estimate and name its source (route source, cost source, emission source).
- Unit tests with fixed inputs; BigInt half-up rounding.

## Resolution (30 Oct 2026)

In progress. Aaron merged (commits `[E3.3]`, 27 Oct 2026):

- `commute.js`: monthly calculation from per-trip sen and explicit monthly trips, arrival strictly before class, cheapest on-time option, unavailable reasons.
- `commute-pricing.js`: `priceRoutes` with sourced e-hailing and driving assumptions and BigInt half-up rounding.
- `emissions.js`: CO₂ from published-factor legs (UK DESNZ 2026 CO₂-only proxy, explicitly enabled) with named sources; mixed-leg estimates.
- `gtfs.js`, `gtfs-journey.js`: bounded processing of Prasarana schedules and transfers.
- `routes-client.js`: a bounded provider adapter for one route request (Google Routes).
- `commute-service.js`: the production request shape `{listingId, roomId, departTime}`, authorised, not exported yet; `commute-preview.js` is the local fixture interface.
- Android: typed handoff and a local preview that shows sources, warnings and the traffic-fallback caveat.

**Gaps:** the free openrouteservice adapter does not exist (the provider adapter is the paid Google one); the UM shuttle timetable (T1.4) is missing; assumptions #3, #4 and #5 are unverified; the five real route probes have not been run.
