## Description

This is the core of Jiran: for a student and a Listing, how long and how much does it cost to reach class by walking, bus or LRT plus the UM shuttle, e-hailing, and driving alone? Aaron has merged the engine and tests: a Routes adapter, GTFS processing, a configurable pricing layer (`priceRoutes`), the monthly calculator (`calculateCommute`), and published-factor CO₂ calculation (`calculateTripEmissions`).

The rules are in `docs/api-contracts.md`: arrival must be strictly before class; monthly cost = integer per-trip sen × explicit monthly trips; the cheapest on-time available option wins, ties by shorter duration; unknown fares stay unavailable, never zero.

## Work needed
