## Constraints

- Units: rent per month, commute fare per one-way trip, travel duration in minutes. Never add them or assume monthly usage.
- No timetable, campus days, current GPS location or arrival-before-class test.
- Unknown fare/time is unavailable, never a fabricated zero. Preserve approximate origin labelling and the selected mode's name.
- Hand-off: [target API contract](../../docs/engineering/api-contracts.md#getcommuteoptions--target-production-contract-fr4143); producer/parser agreement by Aaron remains pending.
