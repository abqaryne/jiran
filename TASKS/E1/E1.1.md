## Description

Jiran is only for UM students, and housing scams are the main trust risk (PRD NFR2). Every account must therefore belong
to a verified UM student before it can see a Listing or contact anyone. See [PRD FR1.1](../../docs/product/PRD.md#module-1-authentication-and-verified-profile).

## Work needed

1. ✅ **Welcome, Sign up and Verify email screens** from the Figma Starting Flow (`ui/auth/WelcomeFragment`,
   `SignUpFragment`, `VerifyEmailFragment`, navigation `nav_auth.xml`).
2. ✅ **The email rule** in plain Java, no Android in it: `UmEmailPolicy` accepts only `siswa.um.edu.my`; the domain must
   match exactly, case and spaces around it are ignored, two `@` signs are rejected so a domain cannot be hidden
   (`a@gmail.com@siswa.um.edu.my`), and look-alikes such as `evil-um.edu.my` or `siswa.um.edu.my.example.com` fail.
3. ✅ **One problem per field.** `SignUpCheck` reports the first problem for name, email and password separately (required,
   not an email, not a UM email, password shorter than 8 characters). Spaces inside a password are kept.
4. ✅ **Sign up through `AccountService`** (interface) and `FirebaseAccountService` (Firebase Auth): creates the account,
   stores the full name as the Firebase display name, sends the verification email, and maps Firebase errors to
   `EMAIL_TAKEN`, `NETWORK`, and so on.
5. ✅ **Verify email screen** with resend (and a countdown) and a check that reloads the account; opening the link and
   returning to the app opens the product shell.
6. ✅ **An unverified account can only reach Verify email.** Done in the app by `StartRoute` (UNVERIFIED goes to
   Verify email). Security Rules also refuse an unverified account (Gathan's baseline in `backend/firestore.rules`, merged
   10 Nov 2026; emulator test "Unverified student read").
7. ✅ **Unit tests**: `UmEmailPolicyTest`, `SignUpCheckTest`, `SignUpViewModelTest`, `VerifyEmailViewModelTest`,
   `StartRouteTest` (64 unit tests in the project on 30 Oct 2026).
8. ⬜ (Ary, Aly) Test on a real device with a real inbox before the video

## Constraints

- **Only `siswa.um.edu.my`** accounts can register: every account is a verified UM student. Other UM domains are out of scope.
- A wrong email and an unregistered email are never told apart on screen (see E1.2).
- First-year students without a UM email wait until they receive it. The sign-up screen says so, and the report lists it as a limitation (PRD open question 9).

## Acceptance (how QA checks it)

Run the emulators first (Auth 9099, Firestore 8080); a debug build needs them. The verification link shows in the emulator log.

- [ ] Sign up with `name@gmail.com` is rejected under the email field with the message "Only UM student emails can sign up".
- [ ] Sign up with `name@um.edu.my` (staff mail) is rejected with the same message.
- [ ] Sign up with `name@siswa.um.edu.my` and an 8-character password reaches Verify email.
- [ ] With the account unverified, killing and reopening the app lands on Verify email, not on Home.
- [ ] Resend shows a countdown before it can be tapped again.
- [ ] Opening the verification link and returning to the app opens the product.
- [ ] Signing up again with the same email shows "This email already has an account. Log in instead."
- [ ] A 7-character password is rejected; a password of 8 spaces is accepted.
- [ ] `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest` passes.

## Not in scope, and why

- **Staff mail (`um.edu.my`).** Jiran is for students. Removed on 30 Oct 2026 after it had been accepted.
- **Postgraduate or other student domains.** Unknown. Dafi asks UM; until then they cannot register.
- **First-year students without a UM email yet.** They cannot join until they receive Siswa Mail. Recorded as PRD open question 9.
- **Sign-in with Google or other providers.** Not in the PRD, and it would bypass the UM domain check.
- **A "link works for N hours" text.** Figma showed one; the real expiry is not verified, so the text was removed.
- **The `users` profile document.** Waits for Gathan's schema (E0.5); until then the name lives only in Firebase.

## Resolution (30 Oct 2026)

Code and tests are merged on `main`. The flow works against the local Firebase Auth emulator on the Pixel_9 emulator
(checked 27 Oct, before the later domain change). Not verified: a real device, a real inbox, the live `jiran-mad`
project, screen rotation, TalkBack, and the sign-up screen after the domain change of 30 Oct. Since 10 Nov 2026 the UM domain is final (`siswa.um.edu.my` only) and the Security Rules refuse an unverified account.
