## Description

After sign-in the student needs one place with the four tabs of the PRD navigation (Home, Rooms, Match, Unit) and a Post
button. The other owners can build their screens only when this shell exists and each tab has a place to plug into.

## Work needed

1. ✅ **Make the shell the first screen after sign-in.** `LauncherActivity` opens `ShellActivity` when the session is verified.
2. ✅ **Four tabs** Home, Rooms, Match, Unit in `nav_main.xml`, each a placeholder fragment, with the floating `JiranTabBar`
   highlighting the current tab.
3. ✅ **The Post button** opens the New Listing screen (built with E2.1, PR #15, merged).
4. ✅ **Profile gate**, built with E1.3 in `ShellActivity` and `ProfileGate`, in PR #17 (open, not merged yet): a verified account without a finished profile goes to profile setup first; the tabs stay hidden until the check answers and stay closed with a retry screen if it fails.

## Constraints

- Single-activity shell with one navigation graph for the product and one for the Starting Flow.
- The product shell opens after verification as a stand-in until profile setup (E1.3) exists.
- Home has no commute card (removed by Ary, 11 Nov 2026); commute to the faculty is seen on Room cards and Room details, and in the Unit tab after the student has a home.
- Home for a student without a Household shows two cards, "Find a room" (Rooms tab) and "Find housemates" (matches), and a link "Join a Household with a code" (E5.3). The Unit tab without a Household has its own three cards (E7.3). No empty screen.

## Acceptance (how QA checks it)

- [ ] After verifying the email, the app opens on Home with the four tabs visible.
- [ ] Tapping each tab shows that tab and highlights it; tapping the current tab does not stack screens.
- [ ] Tapping the Post button opens the New Listing screen and does not crash.
- [ ] Closing and reopening the app with a verified account and a finished profile lands on Home.
- [ ] The tab bar buttons are at least 48 dp.

## Not in scope, and why

- **Real content in the tabs.** Rooms is E3.1, Match is E6, Unit is E7.3, and Home's real content is not in a story yet.
- **The Post flow.** E2 (Gathan) builds it behind the Post button.
- **Deep links and push notification targets.** Notifications are E5.4 (P1).

## Resolution (30 Oct 2026)

The shell exists and opens after a verified sign-in. The four tabs show placeholder fragments and the Post button did
nothing yet (it now opens New Listing). Home carries the temporary Log out button. No dedicated tests: routing is covered by `StartRouteTest`, the
rest by hand. Not verified on a real device. In QA in ClickUp.
