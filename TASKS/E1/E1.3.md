## Description

Matching, commute and privacy all start from the profile. The commute guide needs the faculty, without a timetable or campus-day count;
the match score needs budget and lifestyle; the Host and housemates must see only what the student agreed to show.
See [PRD FR1.3](../../docs/product/PRD.md#module-1-authentication-and-verified-profile) and the stage table in PRD section 5.

## Work needed

   Uses `StepProgressView` and the shared components. Built in `ui/profile` (`ProfileSetupActivity` and one fragment per step); the four-step version was walked through on the Pixel_9 emulator with the Firebase emulators on 10 Nov 2026; the three-step version was walked through on 10 Nov 2026 (see resolution below).
2. ✅ **Remove campus-day collection** (Ary, 11 Nov 2026): per-trip commute does not depend on frequency. Remove the chips, `ProfileCheck.campusDays` requirement, mandatory repository field and `SetupProgress` dependency; update tests. Existing private `campusDaysPerWeek` values may remain unused; a profile without the field must finish setup. Existing owner-only profile Rules do not require attendance data; no Rules were changed. Live writes remain unverified.
3. ✅ **Budget** as a min and max in integer sen (`budgetMinSen`, `budgetMaxSen`); the screen shows ringgit. Typed ringgit is turned into sen by `Ringgit.toSen`.
4. ✅ **Lifestyle tags and "I have a car"** (`lifestyleTags` public, `hasCar` private). Lifestyle tags are a draft starter set until the survey (T1).
5. ⚠️ **Privacy toggles** that take effect at once and are enforced by Security Rules, not only hidden in the UI. The toggles screen (Figma "Profile 3 · Privacy": phone number, budget range, profile photo; on = hidden, all on by default) saves `privacy` in `private/profile`; `showBudgetToConnections` has no Rule yet because no screen shows the budget to others; the Rules from E0.5 read the phone toggle. Checked on the emulator with a second fictional account: `users/{uid}` is readable (200), `private/profile`, `private/timetable` and `private/contact` are denied (403), tested before the timetable was removed. The phone opt-in itself is not tried yet.
6. ✅ **Initials, not a photo, by default** (`initials` on the public `users` document). `DisplayName.initials` (first two words); the photo field is not shown in setup.
7. ✅ **Profile gate**: a verified account without a finished profile goes to profile setup, not to the shell (see E1.4). `ProfileGate` (pure Java, 7 unit tests) reads `SetupProgress`; `ShellActivity` keeps the tabs hidden until it answers, opens setup until every step is saved, runs again when the activity is restored, and fails closed: if the check fails (no connection, session ended) a retry screen shows and the tabs stay closed. Not tried on a device yet.
8. ✅ **A repository interface** like `AccountService` (`ProfileRepository` plus a Firebase implementation), so the screens and
   view models are tested with a fake. `ProfileRepository` and `FirestoreProfileRepository`.
9. ⚠️ **Tests for saving and loading a profile**, and for each current field check (budget min not above max; no campus-day requirement after migration). 22 JUnit tests: field checks, `Ringgit`, `SetupProgress` and `ProfileSetupViewModel` with `FakeProfileRepository`. The Firestore writes were checked by hand on the emulator (every field as in the schema, budget 600 and 650.50 stored as 60000 and 65050 sen); there is no automated emulator test for them.

## Constraints

- The profile can rely on a verified UM student (E1.1).
- Ary, 11 Nov 2026: typed faculty search uses a dropdown with full names, matching names or abbreviations. Always keep Popular chips visible as quick choices, including while typing and after selection; do not create a Results chip section. Editing a selected name clears the selection until a faculty is picked again.
- Fields are the ones in `docs/engineering/schema.md`: public `users/{uid}` (`initials`, `gender`, `faculty`, `year`, `lifestyleTags`) and the private documents `private/profile` (`campusDaysPerWeek` is legacy and no longer required by the product), `private/contact`, `private/photo`.
- Gender: required at profile setup right after sign-up, male or female, shown on the profile. Nothing is hidden automatically: Rooms and the housemate page have a filter chip (Male / Female / Any), default Any.
- The faculty list is UM's 17 study areas as listed at https://study.um.edu.my/undergraduate-faculties (read 11 Nov 2026, page dated 24 Jun 2026), kept in `arrays_profile.xml`. Full names follow the page's link names; only the two "Academy of" names are not verified. Buildings use the seeded area list and a typed name, as in E2.1. Lifestyle tags start from a short starter set until the survey results (T1, 13 Nov).
- No timetable, class start or campus attendance frequency (Ary, 11 Nov 2026). Commute shows one-way time/fare from a candidate Listing to the faculty; true monthly cost is removed.
- Setup does not ask for the name again: `initials` and `firstName` are made from the full name given at sign-up.
- Setup progress is read from the documents that exist (no extra field in the schema): a complete `users/{uid}` means step 1 is done, `budgetMaxSen` in `private/profile` step 2 (remove the legacy campus-day gate), and the `privacy` map step 3.
- A Host needs no extra profile data: any student can be a Host; unit details live in the listing, and the phone number stays private until a Connection.

## Acceptance (how QA checks it)

- [ ] A new verified account sees profile setup first, and cannot reach the tabs until the required steps are done.
- [ ] Closing the app halfway and reopening continues the profile where it stopped.
- [ ] A first-year who does not know their timetable/attendance days can complete setup without campus-day input; old profiles still load.
- [ ] Budget min above max is rejected with a message under the field.
- [ ] The public profile shows initials and faculty, never the name or the photo, before a Connection.
- [ ] Turning a privacy toggle off hides that detail from a second test account immediately (checked with two accounts).
- [ ] With the emulator rules loaded, reading another student's `private/profile` or `private/contact` is denied.
- [ ] `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest` passes.

## Not in scope, and why

- **Editing the profile later.** That is E10.1 (P1). This story only creates it.
- **Profile photo upload.** The photo is off by default (initials); Firebase Storage is not used on the free plan; photos go to Cloudinary Free through an upload signed by the Worker (decided 31 Oct 2026, E2.1).
- **Compatibility questionnaire** (sleep, cleanliness, noise, cooking, smoking, pets, budget). That is E6.1 and writes to `matchProfiles`.
- **Real personal data in tests or examples.** Fictional names only (PDPA).
